Top Traditional Markets in Oman

Famous Souqs in Oman
Name City What You'll Find
Mutrah Souq Muscat Silver jewelry, incense, scarves, antiques
Nizwa Souq Nizwa Pottery, daggers (khanjar), spices, livestock on Fridays
Salalah Al-Haffa Souq Salalah Frankincense, traditional garments, perfumes
Shopping Tips
  • Bargaining is expected — don't hesitate to negotiate prices politely.
  • Bring cash, especially for smaller stalls that don't accept cards.
  • Try local perfumes and incense — they're signature Omani goods.
